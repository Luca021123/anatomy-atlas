✚ Open Medical Toolkit

Free tools for learning medicine, practising it, and staying healthy.

Three toolkits that run entirely on your device. They work offline and need no account, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. They also work on low-cost phones and can be shared as plain files.

🎓 For medical students

Medical Student Studio

  • FSRS spaced-repetition flashcards with ready-made high-yield decks
  • Interactive anatomy: brachial plexus, cranial nerves, dermatomes, muscles
  • Physiology simulators: oxygen–haemoglobin curve, action potential, pressure–volume loops
  • ECG trainer built on a cardiac-vector model, OSCE stations, EBM and statistics maths

Design principles

📴 Offline-first

After the first visit everything works without signal. You can install it as an app or open it from a saved file.

🔒 Private by design

There are no accounts, no tracking and no servers. Your data stays on your device, and you can back it up or erase it in two taps.

📚 Evidence-linked

Every score and recommendation cites its source, and the code is open to inspect and test.

🌍 Built for everyone

It is light enough for low-cost phones, has large-text and read-aloud modes, and supports more languages over time.

These tools support learning and clinical decisions. They do not replace professional judgement, local guidelines, or emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. 112 works from most mobile phones.
