# Public-health evidence brief for an offline-first, privacy-first global health web app

Date: 2026-11-03. Method: web search snippets and WHO/IDF/GSMA/Lancet pages surfaced by search. Wikipedia and en.wikipedia.org were NOT fetchable (network allow-list). Few primary PDFs were opened; many numbers come from secondary coverage of primary reports. Tags: (UNVERIFIED) = not confirmed from a primary/authoritative page in this session, or sources conflict. (BG) = from background knowledge, not re-checked this session. Re-verify every number before shipping copy.

## A. Global needs

### Burden
- NCDs killed >43 million people in 2021 (about three-quarters of non-pandemic deaths); 82% of "premature" NCD deaths are in LMICs [WHO GHO; https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/themes/topics/sdg-target-3_4-noncommunicable-diseases-and-mental-health] (share wording varies 71-75% by edition; (UNVERIFIED) exact %).
- Hypertension: ~1.4 billion adults aged 30-79 in 2024; only ~1 in 5 controlled (about 320 million); 46% unaware (secondary: HIFA); 99 of 195 countries have control below 20%; Bangladesh scaled control via primary care [WHO Global Report on Hypertension 2025, 9789240115569; https://www.who.int/thailand/news/detail/24-09-2025-launch-of-the-who-global-hypertension-report-2025] (46% awareness figure UNVERIFIED).
- Diabetes: 589 million adults 20-79 (2024), ~252 million (~43%) undiagnosed, 853 million by 2050, 3.4 million deaths, >US$1 trillion spend [IDF Diabetes Atlas 11th ed., Apr 2025; https://idf.org/news-and-resources/news/idf-diabetes-atlas-11th-edition/].
- Mental disorders: >1 billion people (~1 in 7); only ~9% of people with depression get adequate treatment; 71% of people with psychosis get no service; LMICs spend ~1.4% of health budgets on mental health [WHO World mental health today 2025; https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240113817] (9%/71% UNVERIFIED, secondary).
- Suicide: ~727,000 deaths in 2021 (~1 in 100 deaths); age-standardised rate 8.9/100,000; third leading cause of death at 15-29; 73% in LMICs [WHO Suicide worldwide in 2021; https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240110069].
- UHC: 4.6 billion people not fully covered by essential health services (2023); 2.1 billion faced financial hardship from health costs (2022); service coverage index 71/100 (2023) [WHO/World Bank UHC GMR 2025; https://www.who.int/news/item/06-12-2025-most-countries-make-progress-towards-universal-health-coverage-but-major-challenges-remain-who-world-bank-report-finds].
- Health workforce: projected shortfall 11 million by 2030 (all cadres); nurse shortage 5.8 million in 2023, concentrated in African, E. Mediterranean, SE Asia regions; high-income countries (17% of population) hold 46% of nurses [WHO health workforce; https://www.who.int/teams/health-workforce/hwf_governance/health-workforce; State of the World's Nursing 2025].
- TB: 10.7 million fell ill and ~1.2 million died in 2024; India ~25% of cases; funding cuts could cause ~2 million extra deaths 2025-2035 [WHO Global TB Report 2025; https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/tb-reports/global-tuberculosis-report-2025] (deaths figure secondary).
- Child deaths: ~4.9 million under-5 deaths in 2024 (2.3 million neonatal); sub-Saharan Africa 58% [UN IGME 2026 via https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/health/3842944-global-child-deaths-fall-but-progress-stalls-nearly-5-million-children-die-before-age-five-in-2024] (secondary).
- Diarrhoea + pneumonia: ~1.17 million under-5 deaths/yr, ~23% of under-5 deaths (older, year unclear) [IVAC 2024; https://publichealth.jhu.edu/sites/default/files/2024-10/IVAC-report_2024_final.pdf] (UNVERIFIED separate 2024 split; check IGME/WHO).
- Maternal: 260,000 deaths in 2023 (712/day), MMR 197/100,000; ~70% in sub-Saharan Africa; 64% in fragile/conflict states; decline only 1.5%/yr since 2016 [UN MMEIG Trends in Maternal Mortality 2000-2023; https://www.unfpa.org/publications/trends-maternal-mortality-2000-2023].
- Heat: 546,000 heat-related deaths/yr on average (2012-2021), +63% since 1990s (rate +23%) [Lancet Countdown 2025; https://www.who.int/news/item/29-10-2025-climate-inaction-is-claiming-millions-of-lives-every-year--warns-new-lancet-countdown-report].
- Air pollution: 7.9 million deaths in 2023 (6.8 million from NCDs); PM2.5 4.9 million; ~90% in LMICs; second risk factor after high blood pressure [State of Global Air 2025 (HEI/IHME); https://www.stateofglobalair.org/news-events/2025/new-state-global-air-2025-report-shows-nearly-nine-ten-global-air-pollution-deaths].
- Connectivity: 4.7 billion (58%) use mobile internet; 3.4 billion do not, of whom >90% live under mobile broadband coverage (usage gap, not coverage gap); closing it worth ~US$3.5 trillion GDP 2023-2030 [GSMA SOMIC 2025; https://gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/gsma-calls-for-renewed-focus-on-closing-the-usage-gap-as-more-than-3-billion-people-remain-offline-despite-available-mobile-internet-services/].
- Mobile subscribers: 5.8 billion unique subscribers, 71% penetration (2024) [GSMA Mobile Economy 2025 (copy); https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-economy/] (UNVERIFIED; LMIC smartphone adoption % not found).
- Device affordability: 4G device ~26% of monthly GDP per capita in SSA vs 16% other LMICs (UNVERIFIED, secondary GSMA coverage). Barriers report: https://www.gsma.com/somic/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/The-State-of-Mobile-Internet-Connectivity-2025-Barriers-to-Mobile-Internet-Adoption-and-Use.pdf
- Literacy: ~739 million youth and adults lack basic literacy (2024); adult literacy rate ~88%; two-thirds women; 441 million in 10 countries [UNESCO/UIS, via secondary coverage; https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/literacy-rate-adults] (UNVERIFIED primary).

### UN 2025 political declaration on NCDs and mental health
- Adopted by UNGA (WHO notice 16 Dec 2025, after contested Sept 2025 HLM; US objected). 2030 targets: 150 million fewer tobacco users; 150 million more people with hypertension controlled; 150 million more people with access to mental-health care; >=80% of PHC facilities with affordable essential NCD/mental-health medicines and technologies [WHO; https://www.who.int/news/item/16-12-2025-world-leaders-adopt-a-historic-global-declaration-on-noncommunicable-diseases-and-mental-health] (exact target wording UNVERIFIED).

## B. Highest-value self-management interventions

### Cardiometabolic
- Potassium-enriched salt substitute (75% NaCl/25% KCl) -> stroke -14% (RR 0.86, 95% CI 0.77-0.96), major CV events -13%, all-cause death -12%; no excess serious hyperkalaemia; 20,995 rural Chinese adults with prior stroke or age >=60 with high BP [SSaSS, NEJM 2021; https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2021/08/26/20/08/sun-1225pm-ssass-esc-2021].
- WHO Jan 2025 conditional recommendation: adults in general population replace table salt with lower-sodium potassium-containing substitutes; NOT for children, pregnant women, people with kidney impairment or conditions/drugs impairing potassium excretion (e.g. potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements); early-stage CKD only after a clinician says yes [WHO 2025 LSSS guideline; summary https://cdn.georgeinstitute.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/lsss_guideline_summary_final.pdf] (exclusion wording from secondary summaries; confirm in WHO text).
- Self-measured home BP -> -3.3/-1.6 mmHg systolic/diastolic vs usual care (2024 meta-analysis); self-monitoring alone ~-3.2 mmHg (IPD 25 trials); with co-intervention (counselling/telemonitoring) ~-6 mmHg; alone with no professional feedback no significant effect [PMC11794135; PLOS Med 2017 PMC5604965]. Implication: pair readings with an action plan/clinician referral threshold.
- Daily steps: 7,000 vs 2,000 steps/day -> all-cause mortality HR ~0.53, CVD incidence -25%, dementia ~-38%, depressive symptoms ~-22%; ~4,000 steps already better than 2,000; plateau 5,000-7,000; GRADE moderate for most outcomes [Ding et al., Lancet Public Health 2025; PMID 40713949].
- VILPA: ~3 bouts/day of 1-2 min vigorous incidental activity in non-exercisers -> all-cause and cancer mortality -38-40%, CVD mortality -48-49% (median 6.9 y follow-up, observational, UK Biobank, n=25,241) [Stamatakis, Nature Medicine 2022; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-02101-w].
- Muscle-strengthening -> 10-17% lower all-cause, CVD, total cancer, diabetes mortality/incidence; J-shaped dose (benefit plateaus ~30-60 min/wk) [Momma, BJSM 2022; PMID 35228201].
- Breaking up sitting: activity breaks every 15-30 min lower postprandial glucose (SMD about -0.3; walking breaks best; acute crossover trials) [Obesity Reviews 2026 meta-analysis; Applied Sciences 2024 NMA https://doaj.org/article/2b7555a05e794703814a7303eec569f7] (acute, not long-term outcomes).
- Sleep regularity: top four regularity quintiles -> 20-48% lower all-cause mortality vs least regular; regularity a stronger predictor than duration (UK Biobank, n=60,977, observational) [Windred, SLEEP 2024; doi:10.1093/sleep/zsad253].
- Diet: EAT-Lancet 2025 update reaffirms plant-rich planetary-health diet, estimates up to 15 million premature deaths/yr averted and food emissions halved [Lancet 2025;406:1625; https://www.stockholmresilience.org/research/research-stories/2025-10-03-eat-lancet-2025-global-food-transformation-needed-to-ease-pressure-on-the-planet-and-save-millions-of-lives.html].
- Ultra-processed food: Lancet 3-paper series (18 Nov 2025) argues UPF displacement of traditional diets drives chronic disease; calls for policy action; "UPF" definition contested by industry [PMID 41270766]. Fruit/veg dose-response: ~800 g/day associated with ~-15% to -25% all-cause mortality vs low intake (BG, Aune IJE 2017) (UNVERIFIED).
- Alcohol: WHO says evidence cannot identify a threshold below which carcinogenic effects "switch on"; light/moderate drinking causes ~half of alcohol cancers in Europe; CV benefits do not offset cancer risk [WHO Europe Jan 2023; https://www.drugsandalcohol.ie/37873/].
- GLP-1: WHO 1 Dec 2025 conditional recommendation that adults (not pregnant) with obesity MAY use GLP-1 (and GLP-1/GIP) therapies long term, with intensive behavioural support; limits: cost, long-term data, access; warns about falsified products; child guideline pending. App stance: clinician-referral info only, no dosing, no sourcing [WHO; https://www.who.int/news/item/01-12-2025-who-issues-global-guideline-on-the-use-of-glp-1-medicines-in-treating-obesity].

### Mental health and sleep
- Slow breathing / cyclic sighing: 5 min/day for 28 days improved mood and lowered respiratory rate more than mindfulness (n=108 healthy adults, mostly students; small, short, not LMIC) [Balban, Cell Reports Medicine 2023; PMC9873947]. Low-risk; evidence modest.
- Implementation intentions (if-then plans): medium-to-large effect on goal attainment, d ~0.65 across 94 studies (BG, Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006) (UNVERIFIED this session).
- Friendship Bench (lay-worker problem-solving therapy, 6 sessions, Zimbabwe, n=573): depression symptom caseness at 6 months ~14% vs ~50% usual care [Chibanda, JAMA 2016; https://www.lshtm.ac.uk/newsevents/news/2016/friendship_bench_therapy_reduced_anxiety_depression.html] (percentages from LSHTM release).
- WHO Self-Help Plus (audio + illustrated book, 5 workshops): distress d=-0.26 at 3 months in South Sudanese refugee women, Uganda; SAEs not attributed to intervention [Tol, Lancet Glob Health 2020; https://doaj.org/article/955a816979774135adb958354d58830f]. Doing What Matters in Times of Stress (illustrated self-help guide + audio, free), Problem Management Plus (5 lay-delivered sessions) and Step-by-Step (guided digital behavioural activation): trials exist (BG) (UNVERIFIED effect sizes). Behavioural activation: scalable, lay-deliverable, as efficacious as CBT (BG, Cuijpers) (UNVERIFIED).
- Digital CBT-I: internet CBT-I improves insomnia severity, g 0.21-1.09 across outcomes, comparable to face-to-face; CBT-I vs non-active control ISI g 0.64 (3 mo), 0.40 (6 mo), 0.25 (12 mo) [Zachariae 2016 meta-analysis; van der Zweerde 2019 meta-analysis] (digital-specific ISI g and LMIC data UNVERIFIED).

### Substances and infection
- Smoking cessation (Cochrane NMA 2023, CD015226; 300+ trials, >150,000 people): nicotine e-cigarettes OR 2.37 (1.73-3.24), varenicline OR 2.33 (2.02-2.68), cytisine OR 2.21 for long-term abstinence, high certainty; cytisine unavailable in many countries; e-cigarette long-term safety unknown [https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD015226.pub2/pdf/CDSR/CD015226/CD015226_abstract.pdf] (no 2025 Cochrane update found; NRT OR not captured).
- HIV self-testing: recommended by WHO within testing services; WHO 14 Jul 2025 recommends 6-monthly injectable lenacapavir PrEP as an additional option; insufficient evidence to endorse self-testing for starting/continuing LA-PrEP (rapid diagnostic tests by providers advised) [https://who.int/news/item/14-07-2025-who-recommends-injectable-lenacapavir-for-hiv-prevention].
- Single-dose HPV: WHO SAGE Apr 2022 / position paper Dec 2022: one dose gives efficacy comparable to two (97.5% vs persistent HPV16/18 infection at 18 mo in one trial); immunocompromised still need >=2-3 doses [https://healthpolicy-watch.news/who-updates-hpv-vaccine-schedule/] (secondary).
- Safe listening: ~1.1 billion people aged 12-35 at risk of hearing loss; WHO-ITU H.870 reference 80 dB for 40 h/week (adults), 75 dB for children [https://healthpolicy-watch.org/new-who-itu-standard-released-on-safe-listening-to-prevent-hearing-loss].
- ORS + zinc for child diarrhoea: WHO/UNICEF first-line (low-osmolarity ORS + zinc 10-20 mg x 10-14 days); zinc cuts duration/severity (BG) (UNVERIFIED).
- Handwashing with soap: reduces diarrhoea ~25-30% (Cochrane, BG) (UNVERIFIED).
- Insecticide-treated nets: ~17% reduction in all-cause child mortality and ~50% fewer malaria episodes vs no nets (Cochrane, BG) (UNVERIFIED).

## C. Design evidence for digital health in LMICs

- WHO guideline on digital interventions for health system strengthening (10 Jul 2019): focuses on mobile-based interventions (client-to-provider, provider-to-provider, health-system, data services); 9-10 recommendations (count differs by source); states digital tools do not substitute functioning health systems; recommends context-sensitive, consent-aware, privacy-protected deployment (BG) [https://wkc.who.int/resources/publications/i/item/9789241550505] (full recommendation list not captured).
- mHealth effectiveness: strongest where tied to a human/clinical service and for low-literacy via voice/SMS; women's confidence/ownership of a phone is a key engagement mechanism; low digital literacy and gender norms are barriers [PLOS Glob Public Health 2024 realist review; https://doaj.org/article/e06152a8c48f439fb8f0e5c6cbb91f3c; PMC4216389]. No 2023-24 review isolating attrition for low-literacy audio found (UNVERIFIED).
- Attrition/engagement in mHealth trials is high and partly education-related [Springer 2023 https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41347-023-00362-x].
- Symptom checkers: diagnostic accuracy low (listed 19-36/38% first diagnosis), triage accuracy 49-90%, emergencies under-triaged (e.g. 78.6% of ophthalmic emergencies); chatbot checkers: diagnostic 14-84%, triage 33-100%; most evidence from simulated vignettes in high-income countries [Chambers/Wallace npj Digit Med 2022 PMID 35977992 (spiral.imperial.ac.uk); Cambridge IJTAHC abstract]. Implication: red-flag-first, rule-based, conservative; never output "you are fine"; always give a safety-net ("seek care if...").
- Question prompt lists: increase patient question-asking when clinician-endorsed and given just before the visit; inconsistent effects on recall/anxiety; may lengthen visits [Sansoni, Patient Educ Couns 2015; PMC12391282 (2025 meta-analysis)].
- Plain-language target: aim ~grade 6-8 reading level, short sentences, icons, audio (BG, common health-literacy guidance) (UNVERIFIED; no authoritative page retrieved). Text-to-speech for low literacy (offline Web Speech/local voices, language coverage gaps) (UNVERIFIED; no evidence retrieved).
- Patient-held/portable records for migrants/refugees: (UNVERIFIED; no source retrieved this session).
- HL7 FHIR International Patient Summary (IPS): minimal, specialty-agnostic, condition-independent "essentials" patient summary dataset (allergies, medications, problems, immunisations, etc.); ISO 27269; FHIR IPS IG v2.0.0 published 2025, current 2.0.1 (STU 2, trial-use, maturity 3); v2 adds Patient Story and Alerts sections; ISO 27269:2025 is v2 [https://international-patient-summary.net/updated-ips-standards-are-now-available/; https://hl7.org/fhir/uv/ips/]. Design use: local-only export of a signed/QR IPS-shaped JSON bundle the user controls.

### 112 / 911 behaviour
- GSM standard requires phones to dial 112 and (usually) 911/999 even when locked/no SIM; networks may reject SIM-less calls (common in Latin America), and 112 is auto-forwarded to the local number in many countries (e.g. 911 N. America, 999 UK/HK, 000 Australia) but redirection is not guaranteed (e.g. Japan) [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/112_(emergency_telephone_number)] (secondary). Design: show device-country-aware primary number, plus 112 fallback, with caveat.

- Find A Helpline (ThroughLine): free, vetted directory, 175+ countries, ~1,300 services; embeddable widget [https://findahelpline.com; https://www.findahelpline.com/get-the-widget; developer API https://developer.throughlinecare.com].
- IASP crisis centres page (routes to Find A Helpline) [https://www.iasp.info/crisis-centres/]. Also Lifeline International, IFOTES (members list on IASP page).

## E. Ideas we must not miss
1. Hypertension loop: home-BP log with offline thresholds, "bring this to a clinic" printout, and non-medication levers (potassium salt substitute with a clear "do NOT use if kidney disease / on potassium-sparing drugs / pregnant" gate) [SSaSS; WHO 2025; supports UN 150M-controlled target].
2. Steps-first activity goal: set a 4,000 then 7,000 step ladder, plus 3x 1-minute "VILPA" bursts for people who cannot exercise [Ding 2025; Stamatakis 2022].
3. Offline conservative symptom guide: red-flag-first rules, "never falsely reassure", always a safety-net statement, uses local emergency number [npj Digit Med 2022].
4. Voice-first, icon-led UI with local-language TTS and audio of Self-Help Plus / Doing What Matters style stress exercises; grade ~6 text [SH+ Lancet GH 2020; usage gap data GSMA 2025].
5. Behavioural activation + problem-solving (Friendship Bench style) and if-then planners as a structured private journal, with built-in crisis page (country helpline from findahelpline) [Chibanda 2016; implementation intentions].
6. Question Prompt List generator: tick-box "what to ask at the clinic" printable/QR, device-local [Sansoni 2015].
7. Portable, user-held health summary: IPS-shaped JSON/QR export, no cloud, for migrants/refugees and clinic referral [HL7 IPS 2.0; ISO 27269:2025].
8. Heat and air-quality safety cards triggered by user-entered/local temperature, offline pre-cached advice (cooling, hydration, avoid peak hours) [Lancet Countdown 2025; SoGA 2025].
9. Sleep regularity tracker (consistent wake time, not "8 hours") with digital CBT-I-style sleep restriction education and a hard stop for referral [Windred 2024; CBT-I meta-analyses].
10. Offline "ORS + zinc + danger signs" child illness card and handwashing/ITN prompts for caregivers; seasonal malaria/dengue prompts [WHO/UNICEF; BG].
11. Privacy-first by design: all data on-device, no accounts, export/wipe in two taps, disguised-icon/quick-exit mode for stigma-sensitive topics (HIV self-testing, mental health, SRH) [WHO digital guideline 2019; HIVST/lenacapavir 2025].
12. Medicine-safety "not for you if..." gating for GLP-1 info and falsified-product warning, referral-only (no dosing) [WHO 2025 GLP-1 guideline].
